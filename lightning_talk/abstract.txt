POWER Integration with the DAVINCI Model Evaluation Toolkit:
Global Climate to Regional Air Quality

Presentation summary

Model evaluations — from global climate to regional air quality —
are built on systematic comparison with observations.
DAVINCI (Data Analysis and Visual Intelligence for Climate/Chemistry)
is an open-source evaluation toolkit from NSF NCAR that makes such comparison
routine: it pairs model output with observations of any geometry (surface
stations, aircraft tracks, vertical profiles, satellite swaths and grids),
computes evaluation statistics,
and integrates large language models to interpret and summarize the findings.
The result is a single reproducible workflow, designed for use in agentic AI systems.
DAVINCI spans scales — global climate models such as CESM/CAM,
reanalyses such as MERRA-2, and regional air-quality and forecast models such as
MPAS-GOCART2G-JEDI and CheMPAS-A — and is used in production within the NASA CERES project
to evaluate CAM radiative fluxes with SARB.

In this talk we introduce DAVINCI's newest evaluation reference: NASA POWER.
POWER provides a single, consistent benchmark for every model DAVINCI reads:
more than four decades of continuous surface radiation and meteorology
at any location on Earth, from virtual stations to regional maps,
on hourly to monthly timescales.
Evaluations that once meant gathering station
records and satellite archives reduce to a single step,
yielding paired comparisons, bias maps,
and summary statistics against a uniform global reference.
And because POWER blends CERES solar with MERRA-2 meteorology —
parent products DAVINCI also reads natively —
every model comparison can be traced back to its source observations.

Which POWER data tools and capabilities do you use (Data Access Viewer, API,
AWS, ArcGIS Image Services)?

API — the temporal point and regional endpoints (hourly, daily, monthly)
with NetCDF output, accessed programmatically with local caching for
reproducible analyses; the Parameter Dictionary underpins DAVINCI's standardized
variable catalog.

What is the impact of your work, project, or research?

POWER's mission is to put NASA Earth observations to work;
DAVINCI closes the loop by putting POWER to work on the models themselves.
The integration provides rigorous,
reproducible model evaluation against POWER as free,
open-source infrastructure.
It feeds model development directly:
surface radiation and meteorology biases, quantified against POWER in models
from global climate (CESM/CAM) to regional air quality
(MPAS-GOCART2G-JEDI, CheMPAS-A), inform the modeling community,
while parent-product cross-checks (CERES, MERRA-2)
could offer the POWER project an independent consistency assessment.
